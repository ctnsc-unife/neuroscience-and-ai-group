COLOURS CoLab & Hackathon
12–16 October 2026
Laboratorio Aperto, Ferrara

Human-centred neurotechnologies for communication, rehabilitation and inclusion. A week at the University of Ferrara that brings neuroscience, AI and human-centred design together. Neural and physiological signals carry information about movement, intention and communication. AI can help find patterns in them, with possible uses in assistive technologies, rehabilitation tools, prostheses and communication systems.

The event has two parts. In the CoLab, researchers, students, healthcare professionals, companies and social organisations discuss who these technologies are for and what would make them usable in everyday life. In the Hackathon, teams turn those questions into technical challenges, working with scientific datasets and with AI and signal-processing methods.

Our challenge asked how early a choice can be decoded from brain and muscle signals: how little time and information are needed to anticipate an action, a question at the core of real-time communication interfaces. Teams worked with EEG and EMG recorded under different levels of effort and uncertainty.

  • Dates: CoLab 12–13 October, Hackathon 13–16 October.
  • Venue: UNIFE Student Entrepreneurship Centre @ Laboratorio Aperto, Via Castelnuovo 10, Ferrara. Hybrid format, with on-site participation required for the workshop activities and the Hackathon.
  • Open to: students, researchers, professionals and other interested participants from different backgrounds. Mainly in English.
  • After the event: a Seed Funding Call for CoLab projects and a Hackathon Prize for one winning project.
